Echo Location A game where you're a bat in a dark cave. You can't see anything. You press Enter to send out a sonar ping that flashes the screen and shows you what's around you for a split second. Then it goes dark again.

That's the whole idea. Three buttons. That's it.

Controls Key What it does ← Move left → Move right Enter Sonar ping (also starts and restarts the game) Enter 5× fast Disco mode (secret) ← → on start screen Switch difficulty How to play Collect green fireflies for points. Dodge the red stuff. One hit and you're done, unless you've got a shield or an extra life.

The screen is almost entirely black most of the time. You have to press Enter to see what's around you. It's stressful in a good way.

If you reach score 150, a boss shows up. He has 10 HP and shoots at you. You fight him with your sonar. He doesn't come back after you beat him.

Power-ups These fall down randomly. Catch them before they hit the ground.

Icon Name Effect 🛡️ Shield Takes one hit for you ⚡ Speed You move twice as fast for 5 seconds 🧲 Magnet Pulls fireflies toward you ⭐ 2× Points Double score for 5 seconds ⏳ Slow Motion Obstacles move at half speed 🔽 Mini Bat You get smaller and easier to miss 🌈 Multi-Shot Fires 3 sonar pings instead of 1 ❤️ Extra Life You can take one more hit Boss Shows up at score 150. First time I tested it, I nearly fell off my chair.

10 HP

Shoots projectiles at you

Your sonar cooldown drops from 5 seconds to 3 seconds while he's alive

Beat him and normal obstacles come back. No bonus points, just the win.

He only shows up once per run. Doesn't respawn.

Difficulty Pick Easy, Medium, or Hard on the start screen with ← →. It changes how fast obstacles fall and how often they spawn.

Speed Spawn rate 🟢 Easy slow rare 🟡 Medium normal normal 🔴 Hard fast frequent Disco mode Press Enter 5 times fast while playing. The whole screen turns into rainbow lights with sparkles everywhere for 10 seconds.

Doesn't help you win. Just looks cool. I added it as a joke and kept it in.

Tech Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no libraries, no build step. Open the file and it runs.

HTML5 Canvas for drawing everything

Web Audio API for the sounds (the sonar ping pans left or right depending on where you are)

localStorage for the high score

CSS for the layout and mobile support

How to run it Download the files and open index.html in any browser. That's it.

Or clone it: git clone https://github.com/aquamystic7/Echo-Location.git cd Echo-Location
